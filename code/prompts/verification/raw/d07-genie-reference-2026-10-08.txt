Q: Why did Southeast net profit drop in Q2?

SELECT `q2`.`property_name`,
  `q2`.`net_profit` - `q1`.`net_profit` AS `net_profit_change`,
  `q2`.`revenue` - `q1`.`revenue` AS `revenue_change`,
  `q2`.`operating_expense` - `q1`.`operating_expense` AS `opex_change`,
  `q2`.`mgmt_fee` - `q1`.`mgmt_fee` AS `mgmt_fee_change`
FROM `thhart_october_2026_catalog`.`revantage_20261008`.`gold_property_quarterly` `q2`
JOIN `thhart_october_2026_catalog`.`revantage_20261008`.`gold_property_quarterly` `q1`
  ON `q1`.`property_id` = `q2`.`property_id`
 AND `q1`.`fiscal_quarter` = '2026-Q1'
WHERE `q2`.`fiscal_quarter` = '2026-Q2'
  AND `q2`.`region_name` ILIKE '%Southeast%'
  AND `q2`.`property_name` IS NOT NULL
ORDER BY `net_profit_change` ASC

TEXT: Southeast net profit fell in **2026-Q2 vs 2026-Q1** because **Harbor Tower** had a **€506,697.15** decline, driven by **€321,489.73 lower revenue** and **€194,852.12 higher operating expense**; its management fee decreased by **€9,644.70**, which only partly offset the drop.
